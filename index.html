Zombie Defense

Zombie Defense is a free top-down zombie tower defense game playable in your browser. A zombie apocalypse has taken over the city - survive all 16 waves by building turrets, flame towers, sniper towers and tesla towers, placing walls and gates to block the horde, and driving armored vehicles for extra firepower.

How to Play

Use WASD to move, move your mouse to aim, and click to shoot. Press Q, E, T, Y, U, I and O to build walls, gates and towers. Use 1-4 to switch weapons, R to reload, G for grenades, H for medkits, B for the backpack, and X to enter your car.

Game Features

  • 16 waves of zombies with 9 different zombie types including fast leapers, explosive exploders and giant tanks
  • 5 upgradable towers: Turret, Flame, Sniper, Tesla and Watchtower
  • Buildable walls and gates to defend your position
  • 7 drivable vehicles from a basic car to a rocket truck
  • 3 weapon sets including the SWAT set and Riot set
  • Armor, shirts and upgrades shop - earn gold by defeating zombies
  • Carl survivors join you on later waves to help fight
