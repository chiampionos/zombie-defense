# Zombie Defense

A free top-down zombie tower defense game that runs right in your browser.

**Play now:** https://chiampionos.github.io/zombie-defense/

## About

A zombie apocalypse has taken over the city. Survive all 16 waves by building
towers and walls, driving armored vehicles, and fighting zombie bosses.

## Features

- 16 waves, 9 zombie types (leapers, exploders, tanks and more)
- 5 towers: Turret, Flame, Sniper, Tesla and Watchtower
- Buildable walls and gates
- 7 drivable vehicles
- 3 weapon sets: Pistol, SWAT and Riot
- Shop with armor, shirts and upgrades
- Carl survivors who join you to fight

## How to Play

- WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot
- 1-4 switch weapons, R reload
- Q Wall, E Gate, T/Y/U/I/O towers
- G grenade, H medkit, B backpack, X enter car

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on a canvas.
